Virtual Reality Interior Design

An immersive VR application built with Unity that empowers users, architects, and clients to easily create, layout, and fully customize 3D living spaces with real-time visual feedback.

Description

🥽 IMMERSIVE 3D WALKTHROUGH
Experience and explore real estate apartments in a true 1:1 VR scale. Transition smoothly from 2D floor plans into completely interactive 3D environments.
📐 CUSTOM ROOM ARCHITECTURE
Generate customized room layouts dynamically. Users can input precise height, width, and depth parameters via a virtual keyboard to auto-construct walls, windows, and doors.
🛋️ EXTENSIVE FURNITURE LIBRARY
Browse through a well-categorized catalog of 3D interior models. Point at any object to display a commercial info panel showing the product code, material, color, and price.
🎨 REAL-TIME MANIPULATION & CUSTOMIZATION
Freely move, rotate, or delete furniture using VR controllers. Swap textures and materials (wood, leather, fabrics) instantly with accurate runtime rendering.
🌤️ DYNAMIC DAY / NIGHT CYCLE
Toggle environment lighting between Day and Night modes. Watch natural sunlight fade as indoor artificial lights automatically turn on to evaluate the room's atmosphere under different conditions.
💾 SAVE & LOAD SYSTEM
Easily save design progress to the local database and reload previous sessions to continue customizing anytime without losing data.

How to use

Use the VR controller raycast pointer to navigate menus and log into your account.
Choose a preset floor plan or create a new room by entering custom dimensions.
Open the catalog to spawn furniture and interior decorations into the space.
Point and click on objects to move, rotate, delete, or change their surface materials.
Switch between Day and Night modes to preview dynamic real-time lighting changes.
Save your layout progress to reload and modify your design in future sessions.
