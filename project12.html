Description

🍬 Sweet Jelly King: Idle Shop
Build your own sweet jelly empire from scratch! Start with a tiny jelly farm, process colorful ingredients into delicious treats, and serve cute customers to grow your business. Manage and expand your shop in this relaxing idle game.
🍓 FROM FARM TO SHOP
Harvest fresh jelly, send it into machines, and turn it into tasty desserts ready to be served. Watch your small shop grow into a sweet empire.
⚙️ PRODUCE DELICIOUS TREATS
Use machines to turn jelly into pudding, cakes, and more. Upgrade your production line to make more items faster and increase your earnings.
🐻 SERVE CUTE CUSTOMERS
Adorable customers are waiting! Fulfill their orders, keep them happy, and earn coins to grow your business. The better your service, the bigger your rewards.
🚀 UPGRADE & EXPAND
Improve your machines, increase production speed, and unlock new recipes. Expand your land, open new areas, and build the ultimate sweet shop.
🌈 RELAXING & FUN GAMEPLAY
Enjoy cute visuals, colorful jelly, and satisfying idle mechanics. Suitable for relaxing and playing anytime, anywhere.
👉 Start small, grow big, and become the Jelly King today!

How to play

Using joystick to move the character around the map.
Go to farm zone to harvest ingredients.
Use the ingredients to cook delicious treats.
Sell the treats to earn coins and expand your shop.
Upgrade your shop to unlock new features and get more income.
Grow your business and become the ultimate Jelly King!
