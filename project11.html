Description

The world of Atomix Rogue is full of heroes, magic, and mystery.
By joining The Atomix Rogue, you will be able to run away from all the mess and chaoticness and discover a new land, Atomix Rogue, where all players create their masterpieces and really engage in the game ecosystem. In this new world, you are on your own to explore, survive and build a new place for yourself with joy through two main characters along with the support of 5 Pets Legends.
Atomix Rogue is an action RPG about the journey of main characters with challenges and requests to step foot in Atomix World.

How to play

Using joystick to move the character around the map.
Tap the attack button to activate Pet Special skill arrows at enemies.
Collect items and coins to upgrade your character and pets.
Complete quests to earn rewards and unlock new areas.
Explore different environments and discover hidden secrets.
Defeat bosses to progress through the story and gain powerful loot.
Customize your character with various outfits and accessories.
Join events and challenges to earn exclusive rewards.
