Auto Hero: Auto-shooting game

You are a fan of the shooting game,
love the fiery gun battles with heavy weapons, this game is for you.

Description

Auto Hero is a side-scroller and 2D platform battle shooting game that combines gameplay with auto shooting, players only need to navigate the character's movement, the firing is completely automatic, the monsters will no longer have a place to hide. Participating in this side-scroller game, you will play as a muscular commando gunmans with a powerful gunfire, evil monsters with challenging missions to upgrade into a super hero. Monsters after each challenge will become stronger, if our hero cannot level up, it will be a great danger to humanity.

How to play

Move control helps your super soldier dodge attacks from enemies
Each time your enemy is down, the hero will receive coins and gems, this is an effective tool to upgrade your soldier strength
Collect the super warrior puzzle pieces, new heroes will gradually reveal
Collect all the quest stars of mission to save the world
